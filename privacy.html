Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Pivot ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Parker T. Jones. This policy explains what information Pivot collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Pivot collects what it needs to route you to the broadcast that matters to you:

  • Account information. When you create a Pivot account, we collect your email address and store authentication credentials securely through our database provider, Supabase. We never see your plaintext password.
  • Your picks. The lineup you connect or enter and the picks you add: spreads, totals, moneylines, player and team props, parlays, and survivor picks. We use these to work out which live game matters to you right now.
  • Fantasy league data. If you connect a Sleeper account, Pivot receives your league and roster information via Sleeper's public API to determine which players are on your team. We never ask for or store your Sleeper password. If you enter a league manually, we store only what you enter.
  • Streaming services you have. The services you tell us you subscribe to (for example YouTube TV, Prime Video, or cable), so we can send you a working "Watch on" link.
  • Saved stream links. If you paste a direct link to a stream for a game, we save it so you do not have to paste it again.
  • What you are watching. A single record of which game you are on and which device, so Home can show what is live right now.
  • Notification settings and push information. How you want to be alerted (all alerts, only important ones, or none; quiet hours; alert timing), and, if you enable notifications, a device push token (via Apple's Push Notification service, routed through Expo's push infrastructure) or, on the web, a browser push subscription. We do not have access to the content of any other notifications on your device.
  • Subscription status. Whether you are on the free plan or Pro. Payment and billing are handled by Apple and our subscription partner, RevenueCat. We receive whether your subscription is active, not your payment details.
  • Delivery and debugging records. A log of which alerts we computed and sent you, used to avoid duplicate alerts and to fix problems.

Importing a Pick From a Screenshot

You can add a pick by uploading a screenshot of a bet slip instead of typing it in. Pivot reads the screenshot and fills in the legs for you to review.

  • Your screenshot is sent from the app to our server, and from our server to an AI model run by Anthropic, which reads it and returns the legs as structured data (player, bet type, line). The app never sends it to Anthropic directly.
  • We do not save your screenshot or the AI's raw answer. Both exist only for the moment it takes to process the request. We keep a count of how many imports you have used this week so we can enforce the weekly limit, and we log counts and error types if something goes wrong. We do not log the image or what was read from it.
  • Anthropic handles the screenshot under its API terms. As of this writing, Anthropic states that it does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models by default and deletes them from its systems within 30 days, with limited exceptions such as content flagged for violating its usage policy or retention required by law.
  • Nothing from the screenshot becomes a pick until you review the legs and save them. A screenshot may show personal details such as a name, account number, or balance. You can crop those out first if you would rather Anthropic not receive them; Pivot only uses the legs.

Information We Do Not Collect

Pivot does not use third-party analytics or advertising services, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We use error-monitoring tooling (Sentry) for parts of our backend; it does not receive information that identifies you. If any of this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, the App Store's privacy disclosures before the change ships.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To sync your fantasy roster and picks and determine which broadcast to route you to
  • To send push notifications you've opted into (e.g., game alerts)
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We do not show ads. Pivot never takes bets, never shows promo codes or sportsbook affiliate links, and never encourages you to place a bet; it only helps you find the game that matters to you.

Third-Party Services

Pivot relies on the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy practices governing the data that passes through them:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database storage
  • Fly.io — runs our servers
  • Sleeper — fantasy league and roster data, if you choose to connect a Sleeper account. We send Sleeper no personal information beyond the league and roster identifiers you give us.
  • Apple / Expo push services — delivering push notifications to your iPhone
  • Your browser's push service — delivering push notifications on the web
  • RevenueCat — managing subscriptions and telling us whether you are on Pro
  • Anthropic — reads the screenshot when you import a pick from one (see above). It receives the image only, from our server, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Sentry — backend error monitoring, as described above

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and the data described above for as long as your account is active, so the app keeps working the way you set it up. Settings such as streaming services, notification preferences, and saved stream links update whenever you change them, and push subscriptions that stop working are removed automatically.

You can delete your account and associated data at any time directly within the app, under Settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, picks, fantasy league connections, streaming-service selections, notification settings, saved stream links, viewing history, and push subscriptions from our systems. One limit: your subscription record held by our payment partner, RevenueCat, is not deleted by this action, and is governed by RevenueCat's own data practices. Standard infrastructure logs kept by our hosting providers age out under their own retention schedules.

Children's Privacy

Pivot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (Settings → Delete Account), or by contacting us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Pivot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
