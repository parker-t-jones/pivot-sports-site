Pivot

Routes you to the right NFL broadcast based on what you have riding on each game: your fantasy roster, your bets, your picks.

Join the beta

How it works

  1. Connect your fantasy leagues (Sleeper today) or add what you're tracking.
  2. Pivot watches every game live and flags the one that matters to you right now.
  3. Get a nudge to switch, timed to your broadcast delay so nothing gets spoiled.
